📊FLP Calculator

First-Author Leadership Productivity Factor for Google Scholar

What is FLP Index?
The First-author Leadership Publication (FLP) Index is a bibliometric indicator that quantifies independent research leadership while detecting productivity inflation through authorship manipulation. It emphasizes first-authorship through quadratic weighting.
FLP = (First Author % as decimal)² × Total Articles
How to use:
1
Navigate to any Google Scholar profile page
2
Click the "Calculate FLP Factor" button on the page
3
Wait while the extension automatically loads all articles
4
View your results in the popup panel
