# Privacy Policy for Google Scholar FLP Calculator

**Effective Date:** February 13, 2026

## Introduction

This privacy policy explains how the Google Scholar FLP Calculator browser extension ("the Extension") handles user information.

## Data Collection

**The Extension does NOT collect, store, or transmit any user data.**

Specifically:
- ✅ No personal information is collected
- ✅ No browsing history is recorded
- ✅ No user data is stored
- ✅ No data is transmitted to external servers
- ✅ No analytics or tracking is performed
- ✅ No cookies are used

## How the Extension Works

The Extension operates entirely locally in your browser:

1. When you visit a Google Scholar profile page and click the "Calculate FLP Factor" button
2. The Extension reads publicly visible information (author name and publication list) from the page
3. All calculations are performed locally in your browser
4. Results are displayed on the page
5. No information leaves your browser

## Permissions

The Extension requests the following permissions:

- **activeTab**: To read publicly visible author and publication information from Google Scholar pages when you activate the extension
- **scripting**: To inject the calculation script into Google Scholar pages to display results
- **scholar.google.* domains**: Access to Google Scholar domains where profile information is displayed

These permissions are used solely to perform FLP calculations and display results. No data is collected or transmitted.

## Third-Party Services

The Extension does not use any third-party services, analytics, or external servers.

## Data Retention

Since no data is collected, there is no data retention.

## Children's Privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
